01 · The work

Fourteen sites, four games, two browser extensions and a book. All designed, built and launched by one person.

New Connection · beta · 18+

Crushhh

A weapon against loneliness — and a tool for connection, of sorts. Crushhh finds the people nearby who want to do what you want to do: trivia night, a sunrise hike, karaoke, the big game. Built to protect you while you reach out, with photo location scrubbed on your device and sensitive details shared only by explicit consent.

New Tracker and calculator · land use

DataCenterDumps

Maps data center sites, built, under construction and abandoned, with their land footprint and the green space that stood there before, read from satellite vegetation data. The calculator estimates how much of a region's land they take up.

New Games

Lucid Lux

The lab's games shelf. Its centerpiece is Sches, a model of how things actually work, whether it's a life, a government, a corporation or the weather: there are no turns. Everyone plans in secret and moves at the same moment, and the board has no edges, so no move happens in isolation and every action loops back around. You act without knowing what everyone else is doing, the same condition the rest of the lab's tools are built for. Alongside it: TapBoom, Resonant and Batduko. No accounts, no ads.

04 — Principles

04 · What it's for

Ordinary life, on your own terms. Taking part shouldn't mean giving yourself up.

  • Be known without being targeted.
  • Meet people without being exposed.
  • Make a living without being disposable.
  • Be described without being reduced.
A

Show it

Bring into view what's working on you: the assumption in a word, the hidden text in a paste, the tracker in a page, the buyer behind a deal.

B

Build better

Make the alternative: ways to meet people, games, art, tools for seeing how systems work, companies that can't be bought and gutted. Nothing you can't understand, question or walk away from.

C

Say what it is

Every paper is labeled as a proposal, a method, an analysis or a hypothesis, with what backs it up and where it applies.

D

Open to challenge

Disagree with something here? You don't have to accept its terms first. Write to b@twl.today with "Challenge" in the subject. Real challenges and any corrections are published, dated, next to the work.

The site practices what it claims: no trackers, no third-party requests, hand-written HTML.
