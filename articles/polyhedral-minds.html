A Personality Test That Isn't a Type

Briar Morgan Greenway · ThinkWell Labs

Personality types sort you into a box and hand you a label. That's convenient, and it's wrong in the same way every time: cognition is continuous, and a type throws the continuity away.

Polyhedral Minds keeps it. At sorted.quest, the instrument renders how your cognition is organized as a measured shape, your myndspecie. A measurement, not a personality quiz.

How it works

  1. Confirm you're 27 or older and accept the terms.
  2. Enter a first name. It seeds your geometry; it isn't stored with your answers unless you make an account.
  3. Answer in segments: single and multiple choice, ranking, confidence ratings, and checks on whether an answer feels settled or off.
  4. Say whether your state shifted while answering, and where most: thinking, feeling, doing or certainty.
  5. See your myndspecie, then read your Cognitive Orientation Report at brief, standard or deep depth.

You can do it in one sitting or spread it across three to five days, with progress saved.

Why 27

Before 27, results may reflect a stage of development rather than a stable trait. The age gate is there to keep the measurement honest.

What you do with it

The report describes; it doesn't prescribe, and it says nothing about what job you should have. It's a shared object for individuals, for coaches working with clients, for educators, and, by opt-in, for teams mapping how their members think together. The Arc shows a constellation of other people's shapes, with pods and complements you can choose to connect with.

Your data

Without an account, nothing is sent to a server. With one, an anonymized profile, your geometry and your report are stored. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no ad trackers, pixels or retargeting. You can export or delete your data at any time.

What it isn't

It isn't medical, psychological or diagnostic advice. If you're in distress, talk to a qualified professional or a crisis line.

The instrument is still being refined, and every participant helps sharpen the model. The full report is free if you leave feedback.
