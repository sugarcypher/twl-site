How to Review a Big Decision Before You Make It

Briar Morgan Greenway · ThinkWell Labs

Big decisions get made under the worst conditions for making them: pressure, deadlines, and other people's interests pulling from directions you can't fully see. Clarity comes from mapping the situation before you act on it.

Start on paper

Before you choose, write down three things:

  • The forces pushing you each way, including the ones nobody says out loud.
  • What you can't change.
  • What the realistic best and worst paths look like after each option.

That alone will show you something you were missing.

When you want a second set of eyes

VolaVue is a structured decision review: one private session, 60 to 90 minutes, for one situation and one decision. No ongoing commitment.

It uses a five-dimension framework called Personal Decision Topology. The first dimension is the forces at play: the motivations, incentives, fears and constraints of everyone involved, including the drivers working in the background. The review maps those, finds the blind spots, and models how the consequences could unfold.

What you leave with

  • A mapped understanding of the situation.
  • Your blind spots, named.
  • Your leverage points and constraints, clarified.
  • The consequence paths, modeled.
  • A grounded basis for deciding.

Who it's for

People facing a consequential decision, entangled in a complex situation, worried about unintended outcomes, or trying to think clearly under pressure.

What it isn't

It isn't therapy, coaching or crisis intervention, and it won't tell you what to do. No prescriptions. The decision stays yours, and nothing leaves the room.

You can start with the intake form at volavue.fyi.
