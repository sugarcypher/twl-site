In-Home Tech Help in Palm Springs: What to Expect and What It Costs

Briar Morgan Greenway · ThinkWell Labs

House calls for your sick tech. GizmoRx is in-home help in Palm Springs from a real person: smart homes, devices, accounts and apps, sorted where they actually live.

Most problems are account problems

Most smart-home trouble isn't broken hardware. It's sprawl: nobody knows which device belongs to which login, which subscription is still charging, or why the speaker in the kitchen stopped answering. That's what a house call untangles.

What I help with

  • Smart-home troubleshooting.
  • Device setup and migration, including data transfer.
  • TV, streaming and speaker integration.
  • App and account cleanup.
  • Subscription optimization.
  • Practical training, so it keeps working after I leave.

How a visit works

Intake. You tell me the problem, the devices involved, the goal, and whether you need rescue, setup, optimization or all three.

Remedy. I come to you and fix what's broken, misbehaving, outdated, disconnected or overwhelming.

Relief. You end up with working devices, cleaner systems, clearer accounts, and a better sense of how it all connects.

What it costs

  • Initial consult: $125, one time. An assessment of your devices, accounts, pain points and goals, ending with priorities, a budget and next steps.
  • Hourly support: $88 an hour, for setup, troubleshooting, integration, cleanup and training.
  • Standard plan: $50 a month. Four support calls, response within 72 hours.
  • Premium plan: $80 a month. Seven support calls, response within 48 hours.
  • VIP plan: $100 a month. Up to 15 support calls, response within 24 hours, and one annual device check-up.

A sliding scale is available for those who qualify. Cost shouldn't be the reason your tech stays broken.

Call +1-424-777-3476 or email help@gizmorx.com. Seven days a week.
