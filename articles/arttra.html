How to Pick Wall Art by Previewing It at True Size

Briar Morgan Greenway · ThinkWell Labs

A thumbnail lies about scale. A piece that looks commanding on your phone can vanish over a sofa, and one that looks modest can take over the room. The only honest test is seeing it at its real size, on a wall.

The paper test

Cut paper or tape to the exact dimensions of the print and hang it where the piece would go. Live with it for a day. Most sizing questions answer themselves.

The faster way

At Arttra, opening any work lets you hang it at its real printed size on a wall beside a sofa and a 5′9″ figure. You see the scale before you buy, not after it arrives. Hover to magnify the detail, and filter for prints 40 inches and up when the wall calls for presence.

Made for the size you choose

Each work starts as one image, painted, photographed or generated, then color-profiled and prepared for print at the largest size it can hold sharply. Every piece is made to order:

  • Large-format fine-art prints.
  • Gallery canvas.
  • Framed and metal prints.

Checkout is through Stripe, and orders ship worldwide.

Pieces you can change

Some works are interactive and algorithmic. You can change the seed, the aperture or the palette, and each one comes with a short written philosophy.

Commissions

Commissions and collaborations are open. Write to help@arttra.art.

I make this work in the Palm Springs area. I begin without a plan; my muse does more than I do.
