How to Find a Plus-One for a Hike, Trivia Night or Gallery

Briar Morgan Greenway · ThinkWell Labs

Most adults don't lack interests. They lack someone to bring. The hike stays on the list, the trivia team stays one short, the gallery opening goes unseen. plus1 is a platonic-only tool to end that: friends, never dates.

How it works

A map shows what people near you want to do: walks, games, classes, outings. Instead of an exact location, you see a direction and a distance band. Use Do to describe an activity or invite others to yours.

Matched on personality

plus1 matches you through compatibility questions about personality, so the person you meet for a hike is someone you'd actually want to talk to on the way up.

Easy to start

Sign in with an email code; there's no password to remember. You can preview it without signing up at all.

Meet smart

plus1 is for adults 18 and older. Profiles are self-reported and the app runs no criminal checks, so meet in public, especially the first time.

Loneliness is common, and it's fixable one plan at a time. Find your plus one, for anything.
