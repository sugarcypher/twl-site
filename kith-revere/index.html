The questions antivirus doesn't answer

Malware scanners look for known bad files. Most of what can quietly control a Mac isn't a file at all.

It's a configuration profile, a management enrollment, a trusted certificate, a proxy, a privacy permission, a launch job, a remote-access tool someone installed and forgot. Kith & Revere looks at all of it.

Is someone else managing this Mac?

Device management enrollment, supervision, configuration profiles, managed preferences and the restrictions they carry. Essential if you bought your Mac used, or left a job that once owned it.

Can anything watch or listen?

Every app allowed to use your camera, microphone, screen recording, accessibility, input monitoring or Full Disk Access, plus remote-access tools, sharing services and listening network services.

What starts without asking?

Launch agents and daemons, login and background items, privileged helpers, system extensions, cron jobs, login hooks, shell startup files and the retired startup tricks malware still uses.

Questions

Before you buy.

Will running the audit change anything on my Mac?

No. The audit only reads settings, files and system records, and writes its report to your Mac. Changes happen only when you choose a fix, read its commands, sign the Release, confirm a recent backup and press Run.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. There is no account and no telemetry. Results stay on your Mac. If you choose to look a file up on VirusTotal, only that file's fingerprint (its hash) is sent, with your own VirusTotal key.

Why does it ask for my administrator password?

Some settings, such as the firewall, the FileVault recovery key and system-wide startup jobs, are only readable by an administrator. The password goes directly to macOS's own sudo; Kith & Revere never stores it. You can decline and still run most checks.

Can the fixes break my Mac?

They can. This audit tool gives you access to components that, if altered, may make your machine malfunction. That's why every fix shows its exact commands and how to undo it, is re-checked right before it runs, and is blocked until there's a backup from the last 24 hours. Make sure you know what you're changing before you apply any fixes.

Does this replace my antivirus?

No, and nothing finds every threat. Kith & Revere covers what antivirus usually doesn't: who controls the Mac, what it trusts, what starts with it and what can see through it. A clean result is not a guarantee that a Mac is secure; it's a careful record of what was checked.

Will it flag my company's management software?

Yes, if it's there, and it tells you who it belongs to when the evidence shows that. On a work Mac that's expected; confirm it once and you'll only hear about it again if it changes. Use it only on Macs you own or are authorised to audit.
