The Trustee Commonwealth of Earth

A proposal for planetary governance in which people outrank companies, living systems outrank money, and nothing needed to live has a price.

Read the paperFull working paper, 78 pages, PDF Download the presentation27 slides with speaker notes, PowerPoint Read it in plain wordsTen sentences and a short story

The ideas in brief

Personal liberties first, living systems second, a guaranteed floor third, money last.
No price to be alive. No one born into debt.
The Rule of Three: no power is ever held by one or two.
