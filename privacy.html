Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Every game on this site is free, shows no ads, and works without an account. An account is optional. It lets you keep your progress, play on more than one device, earn badges and appear on the shared boards.

Playing as a guest

  • What is stored: your scores, settings and progress, kept only in your own browser on your own device.
  • What is sent: nothing about you or your play. When you open a leaderboard, your device asks our database for the public board and receives it; that request carries no play data.
  • Deleting it: use "Reset scores, coins and profile" in the game's settings, or clear this site's data in your browser.

With an account

You sign in with your email address and a six-digit code we email you. There is no password. We keep:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in. It is never shown to other players.
  • The age range you chose (13 to 17, or 18 and older). It is never shown to other players.
  • Your player name and number, which you pick from generated names (a stone and a creature), and the look of your avatar. Photos you upload as an avatar stay on your device and are never sent.
  • Your saved progress for each game: level, rounds, settings, coins, tickets and recent rounds, so another device can pick up where you left off.
  • The numbers from each finished round: score, accuracy, longest chain, level, correct and wrong taps, how long it took, the game version and the time. For Batduko and Resonant it is the cave or round, the score or core integrity, and how long it took. These build the boards, your badges and your daily placings.

What other players see: your player name, number and avatar, and your scores, accuracy and chain on the boards. Nothing else.

What we never do: sell or share your data, show ads, track you across other sites, or run analytics on you.

Under 13

Accounts are for players 13 and older. If you tell us you are under 13, we do not ask for an email address, and you keep playing as a guest with everything saved on your device.

Research

None of your play is used for research unless you choose to take part. That choice is off for everyone and is not offered yet; if it is offered, it will say what is used and why, and you can turn it off again.

Where it is kept

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. Sign-in codes are sent by an email delivery service. The sign-in system belongs to these games alone; no other site shares it.

Deleting your account

In any game, open your account and choose Delete my account. Your name, saved progress, rounds, badges, placings and sign-in record are removed at once and cannot be recovered. Sign out on this device keeps the account; resetting a game on a signed-in device signs you out there first, so the account keeps its progress.
