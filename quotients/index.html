Experience Quotients calculator

Two people each answer for themselves. Each gets a reading: REEQ over SEEQ from their counts, and an experience-over-expectation number for relationships and for sex. Together, the calculator shows how their types complement each other, where they need to agree, how widely their complementarity is spread, and any boundaries one has drawn that the other wants to cross. It does not look for people who are the same. The method is set out in the white paper.

For adults only (18 and over). Nothing you type leaves this page and nothing is saved: there is no server, no storage, no cookies and no analytics, and the page is not allowed to make any network request. Closing or reloading the tab erases everything.

    Appetite comparison:

    Together = 100 × √(Complement × Accord) × (1 + Spread) ÷ 2. Complement is how much of what each of you wants in a complementary role the other wants in the counterpart role. Accord is agreement on the things that need to be shared. Spread runs from 0 (all your complementarity sits in one role) to 1 (it is spread evenly across every role and both directions). Boundaries never enter the number; they are listed below so they cannot be averaged away.
